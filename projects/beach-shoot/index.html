BEACH SHOOT

An abstract visual exploration where light, water, color dissolve into a single atmospheric form.

Visit website Our approach ↗
Duration
1 day
Client
Mia Khalifa
Project Overview

Explores the tension between light and darkness through a series of abstract beach captures.

Key
Insight

Student Context

Learners aged 8–15 lacked structured access to English practice and design tools but showed high motivation when content was hands-on and culturally relatable.

Mentor Needs

Volunteers needed a frictionless signup, clear onboarding, and simple scheduling tools to manage sessions across time zones.

Technical Constraints

Predominant mobile usage and variable internet speeds required lightweight pages, concise media, and offline-friendly components.

Design
Challenge

How might we build a scalable, playful platform that:

  1. Onboards mentors quickly and securely, with minimal setup friction?
  2. Guides students through intuitive, character-led lessons despite low digital literacy.
  3. Facilitates scheduling and session management without heavy development resources?
  4. Fosters an ongoing community and portfolio development in a no-code MVP environment?

Notable
Achievements

Partnerships

Pilot involved two Phuket schools and twenty vetted volunteers, with marked improvements in student English performance. Two international schools joined to offer volunteering as part of their curricula.

Comprehensive Business Model

Adopted a freemium model: core features free, premium resources for partners. One-for-one merchandise sales fund student access.

MVP Deployment

Deployment ready by Summer 2025
